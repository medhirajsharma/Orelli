The Coffee Story

Born in London, Crafted for Your Daily Ritual

The Orelli story didn’t begin in a boardroom—it began in a tiny London kitchen over a freshly pulled morning espresso.

Founders Harsh and Sahil first met as college mates, a friendship that eventually led them to live together in London. Though their careers pulled them into entirely different industries and gruelling schedules, one constant held their days together: the non-negotiable morning coffee ritual.

Living in one of the world’s most vibrant coffee capitals exposed Harsh and Sahil to a breathtaking spectrum of origins, roast profiles, and preparation styles. They realised that coffee wasn't just a caffeine delivery system—it was an experience, a memory of a place, and a daily moment of connection.

When they returned to India, they noticed a gap. While the domestic coffee landscape was growing, coffee lovers were largely restricted to single-origin local beans, missing out on the world's most iconic coffee cultures.

Driven by their shared passion and firsthand exposure to global coffee houses, Orelli was born. Harsh and Sahil set out with a simple mission: to curate, roast, and package the world's finest specialty Arabicas into effortless, Nespresso-compatible pods. An invitation to slow down, explore new horizons, and make your morning ritual extraordinary.
