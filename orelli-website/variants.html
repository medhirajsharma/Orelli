Fixed Height & Scroll Strip

Card ki height exactly 585px fix hai. Serving suggestions aur pack size single line mein horizontal scroll hote hain.

  • • 100% uniform card height across all 5 blends

Variant 3 (Boutique Luxe)

Boutique Luxe & Segmented Control

High-end luxury coffee boutique feel with single-row segmented pack selector aur integrated smart CTA.

  • • Apple-style segmented pack slider (10 | 30 | 55 pods)
  • • Minimal extraction icon grid
  • • Full-width integrated smart Add-to-Cart pill button
