Will Orelli capsules work with my Nespresso machine?
Yes. Orelli capsules are engineered to be 100% compatible with all original Nespresso® machine models (including Inissia, Pixie, Essenza Mini, Citiz, Lattissima, and Atelier). Technically, any capsule machine designed for Nespresso® OriginalLine pods will work seamlessly with Orelli.
Can I make both black and milk-based coffee?
Absolutely. Enjoy your coffee as an espresso or lungo, drink it black, or add milk to make your favourite cappuccino, latte or flat white. Check each blend's recommended serving size.
How do I choose the right coffee?
Start with your taste. Prefer something smooth and mellow, or bold and intense? Explore our blend descriptions and intensity guides to find your match.
How should I store my coffee pods?
Keep your pods in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and strong odours. Follow the storage instructions on the packaging.
Can I reuse a coffee pod?
Our pods are designed for single use. Reusing a pod can result in a weaker, less consistent cup.
Are your pods recyclable or compostable?
Please refer to the packaging and product page for the specific pod material and disposal instructions. Recycling and compostability depend on the material and available local facilities.
